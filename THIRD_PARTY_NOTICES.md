# Third-Party Notices

## APEX-UI

The Mission Core visual layer in `dashboard/` and `frontend/` adapts the visual and interaction language of:

- **Project:** [APEX-UI](https://github.com/RubenM1990/APEX-UI)
- **Author:** Ruben Mouradian / Reznikov Engineering
- **Revision studied:** `f9fd176833a39b4634bad23cdc898fac4e0ab6a2`
- **License:** MIT

Adapted concepts include the gold ring around a cyan particle core, the SVG reasoning constellation, animated path motes, agent overview cards, state-driven visual energy, and the equalizer/status cluster. The implementation in this repository is rewritten for its existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript + FastAPI architecture and is connected to Niumination's real five-agent telemetry.

The APEX name and Reznikov Engineering branding are not used as product branding. Attribution is retained here, in source comments, and as a quiet HUD credit.

APEX-UI also credits two MIT-licensed community components from [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/community/components) for its shader background and overview lamp panel. This adaptation does not copy those two components, but their upstream attribution is noted for completeness.
